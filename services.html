SSD Upgrades

Swapping a mechanical hard drive for a solid-state drive is one of the most impactful upgrades you can make. Boot times drop from minutes to seconds. Apps open instantly. The entire machine feels new again. Your existing operating system and files are migrated cleanly — no reinstall, no data loss.

Data MigrationNo Reinstall

RAM Upgrades

Insufficient RAM causes sluggishness when switching between programs, browser tabs, or running any demanding application. Compatibility is verified against your specific motherboard and CPU before any parts are sourced — no wasted money on incompatible modules.

Internal Cleaning

Dust accumulation is the leading cause of overheating — which throttles performance and shortens component lifespan. A thorough internal clean removes built-up debris from fans, heatsinks, and vents, followed by fresh thermal paste on the CPU where needed.
