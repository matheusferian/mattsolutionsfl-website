Who We Are

Local IT Support, Done Right

Matt Solutions & Services LLC is a locally owned business built on a simple idea: people deserve honest, competent tech help without the runaround.

The Business

Built for South Florida Homes & Small Businesses

Matt Solutions & Services LLC was founded to fill a gap that's all too common in this area: tech support that actually explains what's wrong, quotes a fair price upfront, and fixes the real problem rather than patching over it.

The focus is on computers and networks — the tools people and small businesses depend on every day. Whether it's a family laptop that stopped booting, a small office network that keeps dropping, or a computer so slow it's become a burden, the approach is the same: diagnose first, explain clearly, fix properly.

Operating across Deerfield Beach, Boca Raton, Pompano Beach, and Fort Lauderdale — available on-site, in-person, or remotely depending on what the situation calls for.

How We Work

The Principles Behind Every Job

These aren't marketing phrases — they're the practical standards that every repair and service call is held to.

Diagnose Before Quoting

No repair is quoted until the actual cause is identified. You won't be charged for work that doesn't address your real problem.

No Hidden Fees

The price quoted is the price you pay. If something unexpected comes up mid-job, you're told immediately — not billed for it after the fact.

Plain-Language Explanations

What was wrong, what was done, and why — explained clearly without condescension or unnecessary technical jargon.

Data Treated With Care

Your files, photos, and personal information are handled with discretion. Any procedure that risks data is discussed before it's performed.

No Unnecessary Upsells

You'll only hear about additional services or parts if they genuinely apply to your situation. The goal is your machine running well, not a larger invoice.

Responsive Communication

Calls and texts answered promptly during business hours. You're kept informed throughout the repair process.
