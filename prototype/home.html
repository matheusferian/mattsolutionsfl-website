Both. Hardware repairs are usually fastest as a drop-off. Wi-Fi, network, and anything hard to move gets an on-site visit.

It depends on the problem. You get the diagnosis and a written price before any repair starts, and nothing is billed without your approval.

Yes. Mac desktops and MacBooks, and Windows desktops and laptops.

If a repair carries any risk to your files, you’re told before work starts and a backup is recommended first.
